## What I'm building

### [Playrunner](https://github.com/playrunner/playrunner)

I'm the creator and principal engineer of **Playrunner**, an execution and orchestration platform for Playwright and broader automated workloads.

Playrunner is built around isolated runners and deterministic orchestration, with support for:

- Horizontally scaled and distributed execution
- Playwright sharding and report aggregation
- Managed cloud, customer-owned cloud, and self-hosted deployments
- Secure workload isolation and secrets handling
- Observable workflows with retries, structured logs, and audit trails
- Extensible integrations and marketplace capabilities
- Controlled, reviewable execution for AI-assisted engineering workflows

The longer-term direction extends beyond browser testing into a general execution layer for AI agents, simulations, robotics, and other workloads where isolation, traceability, and predictable orchestration matter.
